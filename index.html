Start in one minute

Choose a scene. Drag a speaker or the white listener. Switch between 2D Plan and 3D Orbit without changing the sound. Click Enable audio at a low device volume to listen.

Your ears are at the listener marker, not the camera.

Read the map

Energy coverage
Where the model predicts more or less sound energy. Ignores phase.
Phase interference
Where equal-frequency tones reinforce or cancel. An undersampling warning means the grid cannot show every fine detail.
Map height
The horizontal slice shown in both views. Source and listener heights remain independent.
Listener arrivals
Distances, delays and relative gains at the listener. Select the listener for all audible sources, or a speaker for its paths.

Colors and numbers are relative, not calibrated sound-pressure levels. Audio uses spatial filtering and output protection, so it will not exactly match the scalar map.

Move, aim and isolate

Drag a source or listener to move it. In 3D, drag empty space to orbit; Shift-drag or right-drag to pan; Alt-drag an object to change height. Scroll or use + / − to zoom. Fit restores the overview.

Bearing aims horizontally; Tilt aims vertically; Spread sets the source cone. The plan cone is a bearing guide; the 3D guide also shows tilt. Solo can isolate several sources; mute always wins. Placement snapping affects pointer placement, not numeric inputs.

Enable Edit corners to reshape the room. Corners must keep the outline convex and uncrossed. Numeric XYZ controls also work without dragging.

Save, export and recover

Scenes save locally when browser storage is available. Export creates a JSON backup; Import loads one. Undo restores a replaced scene. PNG exports the room view; arrivals CSV exports every path, including rows beyond the 48-row display limit.

No scene is uploaded. Audio starts only by request and pauses when this tab is hidden. A stalled worker switches to a fallback. For slower devices, choose Fast map or No sound map.

What this model leaves out

A convex room, distance attenuation, directional sine sources, and one reflection per wall, floor or ceiling path. Materials are illustrative. Optional air loss is a heuristic.

No furniture obstruction, diffraction, transmission, late reverberation, full-wave solution or calibration. The 3D view is orthographic, and the map is a slice—not a volume. Surround presets are layouts, not multichannel decoders. Level contours connect sampled estimates; they add no acoustic resolution. Animated dots are slowed guides, not sound-speed waves.

Read model details for equations and limits, or the full usage guide. No measurements or safety assessments should be based on this preview.
