2D and 3D share the same scene

Drag sources or the listener to move them. Use the height field for vertical placement. In 3D, drag empty space to orbit; Shift-drag or right-drag to pan. Scroll or use + / − to zoom. Fit restores the overview. Camera movement does not move your listening position: the white listener marker is the audio receiver.

Keyboard

2 / 3 switch view. W A S D move the listener in its facing direction; Q E turn it. Arrow keys nudge the selected object; hold Shift to move faster. L selects the listener. Delete removes a speaker. Home fits the room. Ctrl/Cmd Z undoes; add Shift to redo. Shortcuts do not override text fields or sliders.

What the map means

Energy coverage adds path energies without phase interference. Phase interference adds complex pressure for exactly equal frequencies, then adds energy across different frequencies. The grid is a horizontal slice through a 3D sound field, not a full-volume wave solver. Its colors use a fixed −60…0 dB relative scale. Speaker levels refer to a unit source at 1 m; these are not measured sound-pressure levels.

The model uses 1/r pressure attenuation beyond 1 m, a smoothed directional cone, a fixed sound speed of 343 m/s, and valid first-order image-source paths. Reflections must hit the finite wall, floor or ceiling, and both legs stay inside a convex room. Energy absorption is converted to pressure reflection using √(1 − absorption). Materials are illustrative three-band presets, not measured construction specifications.

Near-source gain is capped inside 1 m. Optional air loss scales with frequency using a heuristic, not an atmospheric standard; it defaults to zero. There is no diffraction, scattering, transmission, furniture occlusion, modal/full-wave solution, frequency-dependent wall phase, or late reverberation. High-frequency interference can exceed the grid resolution: heed the sampling warning. The listener probe is calculated directly, independent of that grid.

Listening and performance

Audio starts only after Enable audio. Begin with a low device volume. Preview output is independent of map levels and includes headroom normalization and compression, so it is not calibrated to the map. Direct sound uses HRTF spatialization (headphones recommended); early reflections use lower-cost equal-power panning with path delays. Browser HRTFs and smoothed parameter changes make this an audition, not a measured room response.

Sound maps are cached and computed by a worker, with a chunked fallback. The camera does not trigger recalculation. Rendering sleeps when idle. Animate paths runs a slowed illustration, not a physical-time wave animation. Hidden tabs pause audio and animation; audio must be re-enabled on return.

Saving and privacy

Scenes save to this browser when storage is available. Export JSON for backups or another device. Import validates room geometry and parameter limits before replacing a scene; Undo can restore it. No backend, analytics, external font, library CDN, or uploaded audio is used. Serve the folder over HTTP; it also works on a GitHub Pages project path. Opening index.html directly with file:// is unsupported because browsers restrict modules and workers.
