Info & controls

Yellow marks the selected speaker. The blue arrow is you, facing the top of the map. Drag markers or focus one and use arrows (Shift for smaller steps). WASD and the arrow buttons move you. Reset listener returns you to the starting position; it keeps your speakers. Exact position includes speaker height.

Undo, presets, A/B switches and Restore saved stop audio. Save A + B stores both arrangements in this browser. Restore replaces both, and can be undone. Saves stay in this browser; they are not a file backup.

Spatial audio demonstration; walls do not simulate reflections or absorption. Levels share a bounded mix budget as speakers are added. Headphones can make browser panning easier to compare; device volume is yours to control.
